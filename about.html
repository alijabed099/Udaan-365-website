About UDAAN 365

Strong foundations.
Brighter futures.

A well-structured educational institution dedicated to helping students excel in Class XI & XII Board Examinations, NEET and JEE.

Learning with clarity and commitment

UDAAN 365 focuses on building strong academic foundations while developing discipline, confidence, critical thinking and a love for learning. Our specially designed programmes for SIP students and repeaters provide quality education through regular assessments, personal attention and 24×7 mentorship.

Our experienced faculty guide students through interactive classes, doubt-clearing sessions, academic counselling and personalised support. Beyond academics, UDAAN 365 offers a safe and comfortable hostel facility and a positive, disciplined learning environment.

We provide regular student monitoring and dedicated 24×7 mentor support, ensuring continuous guidance, personal attention and regular tracking to help every student achieve their academic goals.

Message from the Centre Head

A note to students and parents

Dear Students and Parents,

At UDAAN 365, we believe every student has the potential to achieve great things with the right guidance, strong fundamentals and consistent effort. Our aim is to make learning simple, clear and engaging while building the conceptual understanding and problem-solving skills required for NEET, JEE and Class XI–XII.

We believe a good institute is not just about completing the syllabus or preparing for examinations. It is about creating a disciplined, supportive and positive learning environment where students are encouraged to ask questions, learn from their mistakes, stay motivated and continuously improve.

Together, we strive to build not only better results, but confident and capable learners prepared for the future.

Best wishes,
